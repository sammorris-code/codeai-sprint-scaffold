What it does

A district or a regional partner assembles a workshop from blocks that already exist, sees whether the agenda fits the day, and then either facilitates it or hands it to somebody from the facilitator network.

Feasibility note for the sprint: the content blocks for this one already exist in the knowledge base. That makes it the most likely of the seven to actually finish in three days.
