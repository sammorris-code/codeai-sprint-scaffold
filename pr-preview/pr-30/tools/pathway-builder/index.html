What it does

A district builds a course sequence across grades. The tool checks it. It catches three kinds of mistake: a course placed before its prerequisite, two courses teaching the same content, and a plan that needs more instructional time than the district has.

One content rule to hold onto: AI Discoveries and CS Discoveries are parallel versions, not a sequence. Same for AI Foundations and CS Principles. A district picks one of each pair. It does not teach both in order.
