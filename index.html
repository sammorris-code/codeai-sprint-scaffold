District Implementation Portal

One place a district goes to plan a CodeAI rollout. The portal itself is shared infrastructure, not a sprint build. The seven tools below are the candidates. Each one has its own page, and each page is owned by one sub-team.

About this scaffold

These pages started as plain HTML with no JavaScript and no visual styling, so that the structure would be finished before the sprint began. That baseline is done, and those restrictions are lifted — JavaScript, CSS, and whatever tooling your team wants are all open now. Pages you find with dead buttons and forms pointing at # are unfinished, not protected.

CONTRIBUTING.md is the short version of how we work in here — branches, pull requests, and which files are safe to touch.

CodeAI sprint scaffold. Public repository. No student data belongs in here.
